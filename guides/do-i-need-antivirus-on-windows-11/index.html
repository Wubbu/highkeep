Do You Need an Antivirus on Windows 11? An Honest Answer

Updated November 2026

Short answer: Windows 11 already has a good antivirus, Microsoft Defender, and you should keep it on. Whether you need more depends on how you use your PC.

What Defender does well

Defender catches most known malware, updates itself, and doesn't slow most PCs down. For someone who sticks to well-known sites and apps, it's a solid base.

Where people still get caught

  • Brand-new malware. Password stealers are rebuilt constantly so that they don't match any known signature for the first hours or days.
  • Scam and phishing sites. A fake login page doesn't install anything, so there's no file to scan.
  • Things you agreed to. If you click through a warning, run a "crack", or let someone connect remotely, no antivirus can fully protect you.
  • Protections that are switched off. Some of Windows' best protections, like ransomware folder protection, are off by default.

What actually helps

  1. Keep Windows and your apps updated.
  2. Use a password manager and turn on two-step sign-in, starting with your email.
  3. Download apps from their official site, the Microsoft Store or winget. Our safe download guides list the real sites for 50+ apps.
  4. Add a layer that blocks scam sites and checks links, because that's where most people get caught now.
