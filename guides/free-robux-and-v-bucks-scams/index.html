Free Robux, V-Bucks and Skins: How the Scams Work

Updated November 2026

Search for free Robux or free V-Bucks and you'll find generators, "verification" pages, YouTube videos and Discord servers promising currency, skins or items. None of them work. Only Roblox sells Robux and only Epic sells V-Bucks.

What they're really after

  • Your account. Pages ask you to "log in to receive your Robux". The login goes straight to the scammer, who takes the account and anything valuable in it.
  • Your computer. "Generator" programs, script executors and mod menus are a top way to get password stealers onto a gaming PC. They grab saved passwords, Discord tokens and browser logins, including your parents' if you share a computer.
  • Ad money and personal info. "Complete these offers to unlock your reward" pages earn money for each survey or app install, and the reward never comes.

Red flags

  • Anything that asks for your password outside the official game or website.
  • Downloads that say to turn off your antivirus first.
  • "Limited time", "only 3 left" or a countdown timer.
  • A friend suddenly sending a link to free items. Their account may have been stolen.

Keep accounts safe

Turn on two-step verification in Roblox, Epic, Steam and Discord, use a different password for each, and only buy currency inside the game or from gift cards from real stores.
